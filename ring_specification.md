# GOLDSMITH TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
## Dual Enamel Channel Band Ring — DRG-RING-001 Rev A

---

## 1. DESIGN OVERVIEW

A masculine flat-profile band ring in 18K yellow gold featuring **two parallel circumferential black enamel channels** separated by a central gold divider, with engraved 8-point starburst motifs repeating at 45° intervals along both gold rails.

## 4. ENGRAVING SPECIFICATION

### 4.1 Starburst Motif
- Pattern: 8-point starburst / sunburst
- Motif diameter: 3.6 mm
- Engraving depth: 0.3 mm
- Finish: bright-cut (polished tool, no patina)
- Inner octagon border: inscribed, 2.0 mm across flats
- Centre: punched dot 0.5 mm diameter, 0.2 mm deep

### 4.2 Placement
- Located: on both top rail (centred at y = 0.75 mm from edge)
  and bottom rail (centred at y = 7.25 mm from top edge)
- Angular spacing: every 45° = **8 motifs × 2 rails = 16 total**
- Starting position: 0° (seam/join point)
- Positions (degrees): 0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°, 225°, 270°, 315°

## 5. MANUFACTURING PROCESS

### Step 1 — Stock Preparation
- Source or cast 18K yellow gold tube
- Required stock: OD 26.5 mm, ID 18.5 mm, length 8.5 mm (0.5 mm finishing allowance)
- Anneal fully before machining

### Step 2 — Turning (Lathe)
- Chuck on mandrel
- Turn OD to exactly 26.00 mm, true and parallel
- Bore ID to exactly 19.00 mm
- Cut comfort-fit bevel: 0.5 mm × 45° on both inner edges
- Face both ends square, bring to 8.00 mm length
- Polish bore to mirror finish before milling

### Step 3 — Milling (CNC or manual)
- Set up on rotary fixture
- Mill Channel 1: start at 1.5 mm from top, 2.0 mm wide, 1.2 mm deep
- Mill Channel 2: start at 4.5 mm from top, 2.0 mm wide, 1.2 mm deep
- Clean channel walls with flat graver; ensure square profile
- Degrease thoroughly

### Step 4 — Engraving
- Mount on engraving block or CNC
- Engrave 8-pt starburst at 16 positions (see 4.2)
- Bright-cut all ray lines; punch centre dot
- Dust, inspect under 10× loupe

### Step 5 — Enamel Application
**Option A — Vitreous (fired) enamel (recommended):**
1. Clean channels with acid pickle (10% sulphuric, 60°C, 2 min)
2. Apply black vitreous enamel powder wet-pack into channels
3. Dry at 150°C (5 min)
4. Fire in kiln at 820°C for 90 seconds
5. Repeat 2–3 layers until channel is slightly overfilled
6. Grind flush with carborundum wheel under water
7. Final fire at 800°C for 45 seconds (glaze fire)

**Option B — Cold enamel (resin):**
1. Mix 2-part black epoxy enamel per manufacturer spec
2. Fill channels, avoid air bubbles (use toothpick to work)
3. Cure at room temperature 24h or oven 60°C for 2h
4. Sand flush: 400 → 800 → 1200 → 2000 grit wet
5. Buff to gloss

### Step 6 — Final Polishing
- Polish gold rails and outer surface with tripoli then rouge
- Avoid polishing compound on enamel channels
- Use masking tape to protect enamel during rail polishing
- Final ultrasonic clean (no steam on cold enamel)
- Inspect for enamel adhesion, surface finish, dimensions
