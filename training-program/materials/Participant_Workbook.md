**Welcome to the Claude AI Training Program!**

Over the next 5 weeks, you'll transform how you work by mastering Claude, one of the world's most capable AI assistants. This workbook is your personal guide—use it to track learning, document discoveries, and build your personal Claude toolkit.

- Complete worksheets during each training session
- Use reflection questions to deepen learning
- Document your experiments and learnings
