## WEEK 2 QUIZ: Prompt Engineering Mastery

### Multiple Choice Questions (10 total)

**1. What are the three core elements of an effective prompt?**

**2. Which prompt would likely produce the best results?**

**3. What is role prompting?**

**4. What is few-shot prompting?**

- B) Asking Claude only a few questions

**Correct Answer: C**

**5. When should you use iterative prompting?**

**6. What is chain-of-thought prompting?**

**7. What phrase would be most useful in iterative prompting to improve a response?**

**8. Why should you specify output format in your prompt?**

**9. What is the main benefit of building a prompt library?**

**10. Which of these is an example of context prompting?**

### Extended Response Questions (2 total, 15 points each)

**11. Describe a workflow in your department that could be improved with Claude. Include: the current problem, proposed Claude solution, time saved, and ROI.**

**Scoring Rubric (15 points):**
- Problem Description (3 pts): Clear, specific problem identified
- Claude Solution (4 pts): Realistic, thoughtful use of Claude's capabilities
- Time Analysis (4 pts): Specific hours saved calculated clearly
- ROI Calculation (4 pts): Accurate ROI math, reasonable assumptions

**Sample Answer:**
"Problem: Our marketing team spends 15 hours/week writing product descriptions for our 300+ product listings. Currently all manual.

Solution: Use Claude to draft descriptions based on product specs. Marketing reviews and edits (20% of original time). Cycle: Upload specs → Claude drafts → Marketer refines → Publish.

Time Saved: 15 hours → 3 hours = 12 hours saved per week
At $40/hour = $480/week saved

ROI: ($480/week × 52 weeks - Claude cost $20/month) = $24,640 - $240 = $24,400/year ROI"
